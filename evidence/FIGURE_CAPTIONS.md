# Figure captions

## F01_main_contrasts

Equal-participant mean differences with stored 10,000-draw paired bootstrap percentile 95% intervals. Positive values indicate more nonzero-command time during annotated rest. DB5 inference is descriptive after failed development feasibility; DB2 is descriptive replication. DB2 transported and reselected results use the same final people, not independent cohorts.

## F02_joint_requirements

DB5 primary selected-fallback means; dashed lines show 90% onset, 90% offset and 80% coverage goals. Feasibility is assessed jointly per person, not by comparing group means with these lines. All selected methods had 0/8 joint successes. Coverage counts any nonzero command and does not establish correctness.

## F03_command_correctness

Equal-person means on eligible active interiors beginning 500 ms after onset. Correct, wrong-active and zero-command fractions partition each person’s interior time. No pooled frame weighting is used. The MAV fallback illustrates why active coverage is not accuracy.

## F04_DB5_sensitivities

All non-budget prespecified DB5 sensitivities. Stored paired effects/intervals are descriptive; negative contrasts in other settings cannot replace the frozen primary result. Zero effects for 200/250 ms reflect collapsed equivalent selected settings, not a universal equivalence claim.

## F05_budget_sensitivity

All 12 prespecified onset/offset budget cells. These are different selection targets; every development selection remained infeasible and no final participant met all joint goals. Favorable exposure differences alone do not establish an admissible controller.

## F06_DB2_participant_effects

Paired participant contrasts in DB2: transported and DB2-reselected asymmetric settings both increased rest exposure in all 32 final people. Both panels use the same people. Development feasibility failed; these are descriptive replication results.
